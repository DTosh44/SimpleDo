      What’s included

      Current Inbox and other undated tasks appear in every range. Dated tasks use their scheduled day, including completed tasks. All ranges include their first and last dates; custom ranges may cross years. Weeks run Monday to Sunday; months and years use calendar boundaries.

      Completed is a task’s current status, not a history of when it was completed. Returned counts unfinished Inbox tasks currently marked as returned, not historical returns. It is already included in Inbox. Removed tags appear under No tag.

      This view includes all tasks, independently of Planner search and tag filters. Distribution shows task counts, including Work and Home, without assuming how many working hours they need.

      Account

      Use SimpleDo locally. Cloud sync is optional.

      A secure link signs you in or creates your account. Open it on this device.

      Merge this browser’s tasks with this account? No tasks will be removed. Keep local to pause sync on this device. Switching accounts can copy these tasks to the new account.

      Tags

      Work Home are always available. Combine Work or Home with an area tag; Work and Home cannot both be selected.

      Home and separate-time tags do not use work capacity. Separate availability rules will come later. Removing a tag keeps its tasks and capacity classification.

      Backups

      Keep a copy somewhere safe. Backups contain your task details; they do not include login credentials or running timers.

      Recovery includes the saved copy from before your last restore or account merge and the original upgrade data, when available.

      Restore backup

      Merge adds missing IDs and keeps existing tasks, tags, deletions and working hours. Importing the same file again won’t duplicate tasks.

      Replace removes your current tasks and restores this backup, including working hours and custom tags when present. If sync is enabled, those removals also sync to your account. A recovery copy is saved in this browser first.
